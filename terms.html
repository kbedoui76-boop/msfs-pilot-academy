1. Purpose

The Academy organizes aviation learning material for private flight-simulation study. It is designed to work alongside Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 and includes original educational explanations, references to aircraft manuals and public online resources, and a research workflow for aviation-training videos.

4. Research and verification

YouTube videos are treated as research references. Aircraft-specific technical procedures should be cross-checked against current aircraft manuals, official simulator documentation, applicable aviation references and the exact simulator aircraft/version.
